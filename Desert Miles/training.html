Getting Started

Whether you are new to running or returning after a break, the most important goal is to build consistency. Start with shorter runs or run-walk intervals and give your body time to adapt.

Weekly Structure

  • One easy run focused on relaxed effort.
  • One faster or hill workout.
  • One long run at a comfortable pace.
  • At least one full rest day each week.

Training Essentials

Good training balances effort and recovery. Listen to your body and adjust when needed.

  • Increase weekly mileage gradually.
  • Warm up for 5–10 minutes before each run.
  • Stretch lightly after running.
  • Rotate running shoes if possible.

Heat & Hydration

Running in Arizona requires planning. Run early or late, stay hydrated, and add electrolytes on longer efforts.

Strength Training

  • Squats, lunges, glute bridges
  • Planks and core work
  • Light weights or bodyweight movements
