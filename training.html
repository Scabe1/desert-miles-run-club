Beginner Tips

If you’re just starting your running journey, focus on consistency over speed. Aim for 3 short runs per week and slowly build your mileage.

Hydration & Heat Safety

Running in Arizona requires proper hydration. Drink water before your run and carry electrolytes during longer efforts.

Strength Training

Strong legs, core, and glutes help prevent injuries. Try adding squats, lunges, and planks to your weekly routine.
